# Key findings

These findings were calculated from the embedded Power BI model containing **1,413 employee records**.

- **229 employees left**, producing an overall attrition rate of **16.2%**.
- Employees aged **18-25** had the highest age-group attrition rate at **36.8%**.
- Employees working **overtime** had a **30.9%** attrition rate, compared with **10.3%** for employees not working overtime.
- The **Upto 5k** salary group had a **22.0%** attrition rate; the **15k+** group had the lowest at **3.8%**.
- **Sales** had the highest department attrition rate at **20.8%**.
- Average monthly income across the analyzed records was **6,519.53** in the source dataset's units.

Counts and rates should be interpreted together: a group can contribute many departures because it is large, even when its attrition rate is not the highest.
